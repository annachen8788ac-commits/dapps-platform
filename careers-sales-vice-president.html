Sales Vice President

Lead commercial growth, institutional relationships, and strategic sales initiatives for DApps Platform's U.S. operations.

Sales Vice President

Los Angeles, California · United States

DApps Platform is building its presence in the U.S. digital asset market and is seeking an experienced Sales Vice President to lead commercial development, institutional relationships, and strategic sales initiatives.

This is a senior leadership position responsible for developing and executing the company's U.S. sales strategy, expanding institutional relationships, and building a high-performing commercial organization.

The successful candidate will work closely with senior management and cross-functional teams to identify market opportunities, develop strategic partnerships, and establish long-term relationships with institutional clients and other qualified market participants.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute the company's U.S. sales and business development strategy.
  • Build and maintain relationships with institutional clients, professional investors, financial institutions, and strategic partners.
  • Identify new business opportunities and develop commercial strategies to expand the company's market presence.
  • Lead the full sales cycle, including prospecting, relationship development, commercial discussions, negotiation, and closing.
  • Develop a structured sales pipeline and establish clear revenue, client acquisition, and business development objectives.
  • Work closely with management, trading, operations, compliance, risk, and product teams to ensure commercial activities are aligned with the company's business strategy and internal controls.
  • Represent DApps Platform in meetings with institutional clients, industry participants, and strategic partners.
  • Monitor market developments, competitive activity, client requirements, and emerging opportunities across the digital asset and financial markets.
  • Build, mentor, and manage a high-performing sales and business development team as the U.S. organization expands.
  • Establish measurable sales processes, reporting structures, client management practices, and performance standards.
  • Provide senior management with regular reporting on pipeline development, client activity, market opportunities, and commercial performance.
  • Maintain a strong understanding of institutional digital asset markets, liquidity, market structure, and the needs of professional market participants.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Business, Economics, Marketing, or a related field preferred.
  • 8+ years of experience in institutional sales, financial services, capital markets, digital assets, or a related field.
  • Demonstrated experience building and managing institutional client relationships.
  • Proven track record of developing new business and achieving measurable commercial results.
  • Strong understanding of financial markets and institutional trading environments.
  • Experience working with institutional investors, hedge funds, asset managers, family offices, financial institutions, or other professional market participants is highly desirable.
  • Strong negotiation, communication, presentation, and relationship-management skills.
  • Ability to operate independently while working effectively with senior management and cross-functional teams.
  • Strong analytical, strategic, and problem-solving abilities.
  • High level of professionalism, integrity, and discretion when handling institutional relationships and commercially sensitive information.

Preferred Experience

  • Existing relationships within the U.S. institutional digital asset or financial services ecosystem.
  • Experience selling institutional trading, liquidity, market infrastructure, financial technology, or digital asset-related products and services.
  • Experience building a sales organization during a company's market expansion or growth stage.
  • Strong understanding of the U.S. regulatory and institutional market environment.
  • Experience working across trading, risk, operations, compliance, and product functions.

Leadership Expectations

As a member of the senior commercial leadership team, the Sales Vice President will be expected to combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution.

The role requires a disciplined approach to client development, a strong understanding of institutional market needs, and the ability to build sustainable commercial relationships rather than relying solely on short-term sales opportunities.

The Sales Vice President will also be expected to establish a professional sales culture based on accountability, transparency, risk awareness, and long-term client value.

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to join a growing U.S. digital asset platform during its market expansion stage.
  • Senior leadership responsibility with direct exposure to company strategy and commercial development.
  • Opportunity to build and lead a U.S. sales organization.
  • Collaborative environment working with experienced professionals across trading, operations, risk, compliance, and technology.
  • Competitive compensation package based on experience, responsibilities, and performance.
  • Potential participation in performance-based incentive programs, subject to company policy and applicable conditions.

Location & Working Model

This position is based in Los Angeles, California, with flexibility in working arrangements depending on business requirements and organizational needs.

The role may require travel for institutional client meetings, industry events, strategic partnerships, and other business activities.

Reporting

The Sales Vice President will report to senior management and work closely with the executive team on U.S. market expansion, commercial strategy, institutional relationships, and organizational development.

Equal Opportunity
