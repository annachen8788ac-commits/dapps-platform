Trust Wallet & DApps Platform

A transparent summary of current public-source ecosystem context and the Company authorization governing DApps Platform USA U.S. market activities.

Important: DApps Platform-named companies appear in current Trust Wallet legal, trademark, and app-store records. Different records identify different legal entities. Those separate records should not be read as establishing that every similarly named entity is the same legal entity.

What the public record shows

Trust Wallet

Trust Wallet describes itself as a self-custody Web3 wallet supporting 100+ blockchains, 10M+ assets and 600M+ NFTs, with mobile and browser-extension experiences.

Current legal notice

The Trust Wallet privacy notice currently states that Dapps Platform Bahrain W.L.L is doing business as Trust Wallet.

App-store developer records

Current Apple and Google Play listings identify DApps Platform-named entities as the developer of the Trust Wallet application. Google Play identifies DApps Platform, Inc.; Apple identifies DApps Platform Software Services Ltd.

U.S. trademark record

A U.S. trademark record identifies DApps Platform Inc. as the registrant of TRUST WALLET. A trademark record alone does not establish that every DApps Platform-named company is the same legal entity.

How DApps Platform USA is authorized

DApps Platform USA is the Company-authorized U.S. market-facing business brand, trade name, and operating designation of DApps Platform Inc. for United States market activities. It is not a separate legal entity unless separately incorporated or expressly designated in a written Company instrument.

The signed Corporate Authorization also permits DApps Platform USA, for legitimate Company-authorized U.S. market activities, to reference and use Trust Wallet trademarks, product names, logos, service names, and approved brand assets subject to applicable brand guidelines and any trademark ownership, licensing, or usage requirements. The authorization does not transfer ownership of Trust Wallet or other intellectual property to DApps Platform USA.

References on this page to other DApps Platform-named entities remain public-source ecosystem context and do not, by themselves, establish that those entities are the same legal entity or establish additional ownership, agency, licensing, or legal-identity relationships.
