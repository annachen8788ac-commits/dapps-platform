EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP · COMMERCIAL · UNITED STATES

Vice President of Sales – USA

Lead U.S. commercial strategy, institutional relationship development, strategic partnerships, sales execution, and the buildout of a scalable commercial organization.

Los Angeles, CaliforniaFull-TimeExecutive LeadershipHybrid / Flexible
THE ROLE

Build the commercial foundation for U.S. growth.

DApps Platform USA is the independently governed U.S. operating organization of DApps Platform Inc., with dedicated U.S. leadership and Board oversight. The organization is building its commercial, operating, technology, and governance capabilities in Los Angeles for its next stage of growth.

The Vice President of Sales – USA will shape and execute the U.S. commercial strategy, expand institutional relationships, identify strategic opportunities, and build a scalable sales and business development organization.

This is a senior leadership position combining market development, institutional sales, partnership building, pipeline discipline, team leadership, and cross-functional execution.

The role is expected to be both strategic and hands-on, with close interaction across senior leadership, product, technology, operations, risk, and compliance.

STRATEGIC MANDATE

Translate strategy into commercial traction.

The role is expected to establish a disciplined commercial operating model while building institutional credibility and durable relationships.

01

Market Development

Define priority client segments, channels, market opportunities, and commercial positioning.

02

Revenue Discipline

Build a measurable pipeline, forecasting process, performance cadence, and commercial accountability.

03

Organizational Buildout

Establish the people, processes, systems, and standards required for a professional U.S. sales organization.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

What you will lead.

U.S. Sales Strategy

Develop and execute target-market, client-segment, channel, and growth priorities.

Institutional Relationships

Develop senior-level relationships with qualified institutional and professional market participants.

Business Development

Identify, qualify, and advance new commercial opportunities through direct outreach and strategic relationships.

Strategic Partnerships

Build relationships that expand market access, distribution, institutional reach, and commercial credibility.

Pipeline & Forecasting

Establish structured pipeline management, forecasting, reporting, and performance metrics.

Client Leadership

Maintain high standards of responsiveness, executive communication, and long-term relationship management.

Commercial Organization

Recruit, develop, and lead sales and business development capabilities as the organization grows.

Cross-Functional Execution

Coordinate with product, technology, operations, risk, and compliance so commercial priorities remain executable and appropriately governed.

QUALIFICATIONS

Experience that drives commercial growth.

  • 10+ years of relevant experience in institutional sales, business development, financial services, capital markets, digital assets, fintech, or a related field.
  • Demonstrated track record of developing senior-level institutional relationships.
  • Proven ability to generate business and build a measurable commercial pipeline.
  • Experience leading sales, business development, or broader commercial teams.
  • Strong understanding of institutional client requirements and financial-market dynamics.
  • Excellent negotiation, presentation, communication, and relationship-management skills.
  • Ability to operate effectively with senior leadership and cross-functional teams.
  • High level of professionalism, integrity, discretion, judgment, and accountability.
SUCCESS IN THIS ROLE

Build the pipeline. Grow the network. Scale the team.

01

Market Presence

Increase commercial visibility and relevance across priority U.S. institutional segments.

Client Growth

FIRST 12 MONTHS

Establish the commercial foundation.

Scale business development activity, deepen strategic relationships, refine repeatable commercial processes, and define broader team-build requirements.
