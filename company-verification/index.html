Company Verification

A clear statement of the legal entity, U.S. business designation, authorized representatives, and supporting corporate authorization for United States market activities.

OUR APPROACH

Credibility begins with careful disclosure.

DApps Platform Inc., a Delaware corporation, is the legal entity for applicable U.S. employment, contracting, and corporate obligations.

DApps Platform USA is the Company-authorized U.S. market-facing business brand, trade name, and operating designation of DApps Platform Inc. It does not constitute a separate legal entity unless separately incorporated or expressly designated in a written Company instrument.

Where the DApps Platform USA Board is referenced in executive or governance materials, it functions as an internal U.S. governance and oversight body under Company-authorized U.S. market operations; it is not the board of a separately incorporated DApps Platform USA entity.

The signed Corporate Authorization effective September 3, 2026 confirms the U.S. business designation, recruitment and contracting framework, and the authority described for U.S. executive employment agreement execution.
