What the public record shows

Trust Wallet

Trust Wallet describes itself as a self-custody Web3 wallet supporting 100+ blockchains, 10M+ assets and 600M+ NFTs. Its current website also promotes mobile and browser-extension experiences.

Current legal notice

The Trust Wallet privacy notice currently states that Dapps Platform Bahrain W.L.L is doing business as Trust Wallet.

App-store developer records

Current Apple and Google Play listings identify DApps Platform-named entities as the developer of the Trust Wallet application. Google Play currently identifies DApps Platform, Inc.; Apple identifies DApps Platform Software Services Ltd.

U.S. trademark record

A U.S. trademark record identifies DApps Platform Inc. as the registrant of TRUST WALLET. This is useful corporate context, but a trademark record alone does not establish that every DApps Platform-named company is the same legal entity.

DApps Platform USA is presented on this site as a U.S.-focused operation in development, based in Los Angeles. The site can reference the Trust Wallet ecosystem and publicly documented DApps Platform entities, but it should not imply a legal identity, ownership structure, licensing relationship, or agency relationship that has not been documented.
