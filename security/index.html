OUR APPROACH

Security is an operating model, not a feature.

Digital financial infrastructure requires more than technical controls. It requires clear ownership, disciplined access, separation of duties, monitored operations, resilient architecture, documented response procedures, and independent challenge.

DApps Platform is developing these capabilities alongside the technology itself so that security and risk controls can scale with the organization rather than being added after growth.

Current stage: These principles describe the security and control framework being developed for the U.S. organization. They should not be read as a claim that every planned system, certification, control, or service is already operational.
CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Four layers of institutional discipline

A practical framework covering identity, technology, operations, and governance.

01

Identity & Access

Least-privilege access, role-based authorization, strong authentication, privileged-access discipline, and periodic access review.

02

Platform & Data Security

Secure architecture, data protection, environment separation, dependency oversight, change controls, and security-minded engineering practices.

03

Operational Resilience

Monitoring, exception management, incident readiness, business continuity concepts, vendor oversight, and documented operational procedures.

04

Governance & Assurance

Defined accountability, policy governance, risk assessment, escalation, independent review, remediation tracking, and management reporting.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Designed for professional scrutiny.

Our objective is to make security decisions understandable, reviewable, and accountable.

01

Minimize Trust

Access and authority should be deliberately scoped rather than broadly assumed.

02

Separate Critical Duties

Material workflows should avoid unnecessary concentration of control in a single individual or process.

03

Make Activity Observable

Important system and operational events should generate enough visibility to support detection, review, and investigation.

04

Prepare for Failure

Resilience depends on planning for disruption, recovery, escalation, and controlled restoration.

05

Review Material Risk

Risk decisions should be documented, challenged where appropriate, and revisited as the business changes.

06

Build for Scale

Controls should evolve with transaction volume, organizational complexity, technology dependencies, and regulatory expectations.

GOVERNANCE

Clear ownership. Independent challenge.

Security and risk management are intended to operate within a broader governance structure that includes executive accountability, technology ownership, risk and compliance oversight, and independent assurance as those functions are established.

Material issues should have clear owners, documented remediation priorities, escalation paths, and visibility appropriate to their potential impact.
