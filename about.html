Building the Company Behind the Infrastructure

DApps Platform is an emerging technology company focused on digital assets, Web3 infrastructure, financial market workflows, and institutional-grade operating principles.

Company status: DApps Platform is currently in the build-out phase. Team, product capabilities, operating processes, and corporate infrastructure are being developed.

Our Approach

We are building a platform around a simple idea: digital finance needs infrastructure that is reliable, security-minded, operationally disciplined, and designed for the realities of professional markets.

Technology

Our roadmap brings together digital asset infrastructure, Web3 technology, market connectivity, risk controls, and institutional workflows. The platform is being developed in stages rather than presented as a finished product before it is ready.

What We Are Building Now

The current phase is focused on establishing the U.S. operating foundation: corporate and compliance processes, technology architecture, security controls, hiring, vendor relationships, and a disciplined product roadmap. Public-facing materials are intentionally written to distinguish planned capabilities from services that are already live.

Trust Wallet & Public Records

Trust Wallet is a major self-custody Web3 wallet brand. Current public materials describe support for more than 100 blockchains, 10M+ digital assets, and 600M+ NFTs. Current app-store listings also market the wallet to more than 200 million users. Separately, public legal and app-store records connect the Trust Wallet brand and software to multiple DApps Platform-named entities.

For clarity, this website uses the phrase “Trust Wallet ecosystem context” rather than asserting that DApps Platform USA is identical to, or the legal operator of, every entity associated with Trust Wallet. Any future corporate relationship will be described according to supporting legal documentation.
