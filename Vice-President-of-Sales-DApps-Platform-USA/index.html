EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP · UNITED STATES

Sales Vice President – USA

Lead the Company's U.S. commercial strategy, institutional relationships, business development, and sales organization as DApps Platform expands its presence in the United States.

Los Angeles, California Full-Time Executive Leadership Hybrid / Flexible
THE ROLE

Build the commercial foundation for U.S. growth.

DApps Platform is building its U.S. organization in Los Angeles and developing the commercial, operational, technology, and governance capabilities required for its next stage of growth.

The Sales Vice President – USA will be responsible for developing and executing the Company's U.S. commercial strategy, expanding institutional relationships, identifying new business opportunities, and building a scalable sales organization.

This is a senior leadership position combining strategic planning, institutional relationship development, business development, sales execution, team leadership, and cross-functional coordination.

The successful candidate will work closely with senior leadership and teams across trading, operations, technology, risk, compliance, and product to ensure commercial priorities are aligned with the Company's broader business strategy and operating standards.

The role is expected to be both strategic and hands-on. The successful candidate should be comfortable establishing relationships personally, developing new opportunities, building sales processes, and creating the organizational structure required to support long-term growth.

STRATEGIC MANDATE

Lead the next stage of commercial development.

The Sales Vice President will help translate the Company's U.S. expansion strategy into measurable commercial growth, institutional relationships, and a scalable client development organization.

02

03

Organizational Buildout

Build the people, processes, systems, and standards required for a professional U.S. sales organization.
