LEADERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

Building leadership with accountability.

DApps Platform is developing the leadership, governance, risk, technology, and commercial functions required to support a professional U.S. organization.

CURRENT DISCLOSURE

Roles before announcements.

Individual leadership appointments and biographies will be published when appointments are finalized and the relevant information has been approved for public disclosure.

Until then, this page describes the leadership functions being established rather than presenting names, titles, affiliations, or credentials that have not been formally announced.

This approach is intended to preserve accuracy, confidentiality, and appropriate governance during the organization's development.

LEADERSHIP FUNCTIONS

Capabilities being established.

The developing operating model is intended to provide clear ownership, independent oversight, and disciplined execution.

01

Executive Leadership

Enterprise priorities, organizational development, capital allocation, operating discipline, and accountability for the U.S. strategy.

02

Governance & Internal Audit

Independent, risk-based assurance; governance standards; internal controls; issue reporting; and remediation oversight.

03

Risk & Compliance

Risk assessment, policy development, regulatory analysis, control design, escalation, and alignment with applicable requirements.

04

Technology & Security

Architecture, resilience, information security, platform controls, vendor oversight, and responsible technical development.

05

Commercial Leadership

Institutional relationships, market development, client standards, strategic partnerships, and disciplined commercial execution.

06

People & Operations

Organizational design, talent, workplace practices, operating processes, documentation, and scalable administrative capabilities.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Standards for responsible leadership.

A

Clear Accountability

Defined responsibility, decision rights, escalation paths, and measurable ownership of material priorities.

B

Independent Challenge

Appropriate separation of oversight functions and constructive challenge of material risks, controls, and decisions.

C

Accurate Disclosure

External statements should reflect information that is finalized, supportable, and approved for public communication.
